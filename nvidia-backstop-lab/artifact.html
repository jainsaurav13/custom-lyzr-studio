The projection

The workbook is a single year held flat forever. Almost every real risk in this structure lives in the shape of years 2 through 8 — rental price compression, contract roll-off, and the amortisation schedule.

Sensitivity

Two drivers dominate everything: what happens to the rental price, and how full the fleet stays. Pick an output and read the surface.

Monte Carlo

Price decay and utilisation are drawn together from a common demand factor, so bad price outcomes arrive alongside bad utilisation — which is the whole point. A guarantee written against uncorrelated risks is cheap; this one is not.
